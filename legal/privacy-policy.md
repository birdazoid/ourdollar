This Privacy Policy explains how [[LEGAL_ENTITY]] (“we,” “us,” “our”) collects,
uses, and shares information when you use the OurDollar mobile app and related
services (the “Service”). By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

OurDollar is a household budgeting tool. It helps you and the people you share
money with track income, bills, savings goals, and weekly spending. It is **not**
a bank, does not connect to your bank accounts, and does not move, hold, or
transfer money.

---

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you provide:**

- **Account information** — your email address and a password. Passwords are
  handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form; we
  never see your plain-text password.
- **Household and budgeting information you enter** — household name; the names
  and chosen avatars of household members; income sources and amounts; bills;
  savings goals; transactions and spending you log; and “fun money” allocations.
  You choose what to enter. This information is financial in nature but is
  entered by you, not gathered from financial institutions.
- **Invitation details** — if you invite someone to your household, the email
  address you enter for them, so we can send the invitation.
- **Communication preferences** — whether you have opted in to product-update
  emails.

**Information collected automatically:**

- **Push notification tokens and device platform** — if you enable
  notifications, a device token used to deliver spend alerts and reminders, and
  whether your device is iOS or Android.

We do **not** use third-party advertising networks, and we do **not** embed
third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information to:

- Provide and operate the Service (store and sync your household’s budget across
  devices and members);
- Send **push notifications** you’ve enabled, such as spend alerts and bill
  reminders;
- Send **transactional emails**, such as household invitations and
  account-related messages;
- Send **product-update emails only if you have opted in** (you can opt out at
  any time in the app or via the unsubscribe link);
- Maintain the security and integrity of the Service;
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 3. How information is shared

**Within your household.** OurDollar is a shared tool. Information you enter
into a household (income, bills, goals, transactions, member names, etc.) is
visible to the other members of that household. Only invite people you intend
to share this information with.

**Service providers (subprocessors).** We share information with vendors who
process it on our behalf, under contract, solely to provide the Service:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and hosting (stores your account and
  household data).
- **Expo** — delivery of push notifications.
- **Resend** — delivery of transactional emails (such as household invitations).
- **Apple / Google** — app distribution through their app stores, and, if paid
  features are offered in the future, processing of purchases (we would not
  receive your full payment card details).

**Legal reasons.** We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect
the rights, safety, or property of users, the public, or us.

**We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not share it for
cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 4. Payments

The current version of the Service does not charge you and does not collect
payment information. If subscriptions or paid features are offered in the
future, purchases would be processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play, and
those platforms — not us — would handle your payment details, subject to their
own privacy policies.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. **You can delete
your account at any time** in the app (Profile → Account → Delete account).
Deleting your account permanently removes your account, any households you own
and their data (bills, goals, income, transactions, and history), and your push
tokens; your membership in households owned by other people is removed. Some
information may persist for a limited time in encrypted backups before being
overwritten, and we may retain limited records where required by law.

## 6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including
encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls that restrict each
household’s data to its members. No method of transmission or storage is 100%
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain
processing. You can:

- **Access and edit** most of your information directly in the app;
- **Delete** your account and its data in the app;
- **Opt out** of product-update emails in the app or via the unsubscribe link.

To make a rights request, contact us at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. We will respond as
required by applicable law. If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/

with your data protection authority and the right not to receive discriminatory
treatment for exercising your rights.

## 8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under [[MIN_AGE]], and children do not
create accounts. An adult account holder may add a child to a household as a
“fun-money-only” member (a name and allowance), but this does not create an
account or a login for that child. If you believe a child has provided us
personal information in a way that requires action, contact us at
[[CONTACT_EMAIL]].

access the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your
information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may
have different data-protection laws than your country.
